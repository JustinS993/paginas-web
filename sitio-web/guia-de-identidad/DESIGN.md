## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the balance between ancestral butchery heritage and modern gastronomic curation. The identity communicates mastery, origin, craftsmanship, and uncompromising quality. The visual language evokes the atmosphere of an upscale European carnicería merged with a contemporary culinary studio: thick butcher blocks, hand-stamped craft paper, aged brass accents, and pristine marble prep surfaces.

The design movement combines **Contemporary Editorial Heritage** with **Warm Tactile Minimalism**. It relies on generous, confident typography, structural borders inspired by classic ledger books and artisanal packaging, tactile tag components, and an orderly presentation of cuts, aging times, and provenance.

## Colors

The palette establishes warmth, organic authority, and culinary appetite appeal:

- **Primary (`#881337` - Oxblood / Deep Burgundy):** Reserved for focal calls to action, badges of heritage, active tab indicators, and editorial accents. For pressed or deep interaction states, deep burgundy transitions to `#4C0519`.
- **Secondary (`#1C1917` - Slate Charcoal):** Delivers structural grounding. Used for display titles, solid primary interaction containers, key icons, and dark framing sections.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706` - Warm Amber / Brass Gold):** Emphasizes certifications, star ratings, cut quality tiers (e.g., Dry Aged, Prime, Madurado), and seasonal alerts.
- **Neutral (`#FDFBF7` - Warm Cream Paper):** Forms the foundational surface background. Secondary surface tiers leverage `#F5F2EB`, while subtle dividing lines use `#E7E0D3`. Text elements default to `#1C1917` for titles and `#44403C` for legible body text.
- **Semantic WhatsApp Accent (`#15803D` / `#166534`):** A dedicated, controlled green reserved exclusively for direct WhatsApp ordering actions to preserve native channel recognition while respecting the artisanal tone.

## Typography

Typography pairs **Epilogue** for display and editorial hierarchy with **Manrope** for informational density and e-commerce readability:

- **Epilogue** carries distinctive, sculpted character forms reminiscent of traditional craft stamps and artisanal butcher signage without sacrificing clean modern geometry.
- **Manrope** provides optical balance and high legibility across product specifications (weight variations, aging days, pricing per kg/lb, and cooking recommendations).
- **Label Stamp (`label-stamp`):** Always transformed to uppercase with extended tracking (`letterSpacing: 0.12em`), providing an authentic butchery quality seal aesthetic.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a balanced, fluid 12-column grid on desktop, scaling to 6 columns on tablet, and 4 columns on mobile.

- **Desktop (min-width: 1024px):** A constrained maximum canvas width of `1280px` maintains an editorial catalog feel. Outer margin is `margin: 3rem`, with `gutter: 1.5rem`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** Fluid container with outer margin set to `2rem` and gutters at `1.25rem`.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Content expands across the viewport using `margin-mobile: 1.25rem` and `gutter-mobile: 1rem` to maximize imagery and catalog readability.
- **Rhythm & Flow:** Information density is intentional. Cuts and pantry goods are displayed in structured grid modules with consistent vertical padding, anchored by generous breathing room around section breaks (`space-xl`).

## Elevation & Depth

The design system achieves hierarchy through **Tonal Surface Layering** paired with **Low-Contrast Organic Outlines** rather than synthetic floating drop-shadows:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - Base Ground: `#FDFBF7` (Canvas).
  - Raised Cards & Modals: `#FFFFFF`.
  - Inset Fields & Recessed Areas: `#F5F2EB`.
  - Contrast Shelves (Special reserves & cuts of the day): `#1C1917` with light text.
- **Border Treatment:** Elements rely on razor-thin borders (`1px solid #E7E0D3` on light surfaces; `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` on slate surfaces) to emulate bespoke packaging lines.
- **Ambient Shadow:** For interactive floating menus, cart summaries, and WhatsApp floating actions, elevation is provided by an ultra-soft, warm umber shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

The design system uses soft, refined corners (`roundedness: 1`), honoring the geometric precision of classic culinary cards, butcher tags, and butcher block proportions without appearing sharp or unapproachable.

- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Cards):** `0.25rem` (4px).
- **Surface Panels & Modals (`rounded-lg`):** `0.5rem` (8px).
- **Featured Badges & Seals:** Circular or clipped tag motifs, balanced with precise rectangular boundaries.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid oxblood (`#881337`) with text in cream (`#FDFBF7`). Border-radius of `4px`. Subtle transition to dark burgundy (`#700F2B`) on hover.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Outlined in slate charcoal (`#1C1917`) with transparent background, tracking labels in uppercase with `label-lg`.
- **WhatsApp Action Button:** Dedicated high-priority CTA with background `#15803D`, white label, and WhatsApp emblem. Incorporates quick order metadata (e.g., "Pedir por WhatsApp").

### Cut Badges & Quality Chips
- **Quality Seal Chip:** Amber background (`#FEF3C7`) with warm gold/amber border (`#D97706`) and typography in `label-stamp` style. Used for attributes like "Maduración 45 Días", "Origen Certificado", or "Corte Premium".
- **Filter Chips:** Cream background (`#F5F2EB`), border in `#E7E0D3`. Active state toggles to solid `#1C1917` with crisp white text.

### Product & Cut Cards
- Crisp white surface (`#FFFFFF`) framed by a subtle border (`1px solid #E7E0D3`).
- High-contrast photographic frame with subtle inset border.
- Metadata block detailing cooking method icons (Parrilla, Horno, Sartén), thickness/weight, and origin stamp.
- Direct dual actions: "Añadir al Carrito" and an instant "Pedir Corte por WhatsApp" direct tap.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background `#F5F2EB`, border `1px solid #D6CEBE`. Focus state shifts to `1px solid #881337` with a matching focus ring.
- Weight selectors use segmented buttons with clear kilogram and portion unit toggles.

### Lists & Ledger Specs
- Butcher table specifications (Rendimiento, Grasa intramuscular, Maduración) rendered with dashed dotted leaders (`border-bottom: 1px dotted #D6CEBE`) evoking traditional butcher ledgers.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square with soft 2px edges. Checked state in `#881337` with an ivory checkmark. Accent radio buttons feature a concentric oxblood target ring.
